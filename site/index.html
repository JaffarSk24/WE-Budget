A budget built around your payment calendar

Plan every payment and income of the month, set money aside in envelopes as soon as income lands, and tick payments off when they happen. WE Budget always shows how much money is really free and which upcoming payment there will not be enough money for.

Download for macOS and Windows How to install

Free and open source, MIT license. English and Russian.

What it does

Payment calendar

Each month is a list of payments and incomes with a status: planned, set aside, done or cancelled. Payments still waiting for money stand out.

Envelopes

Accounts hold envelopes, like the spaces of a banking app. Money set aside for a payment never counts as free money for anything else.

Splitting income

When income arrives, the app proposes how much to move to each envelope to cover the payments until the next income.

Checking against the bank

Type what your bank shows, and any difference becomes a visible adjustment, so balances and the forecast stay true.

Recurring payments

Monthly, every few months, yearly, weekly or once. The plan for the coming months builds itself.

Sync between computers

Optional sync through a hidden app folder of your own Google Drive. Works offline and catches up later.

Your data stays yours

Your budget is kept in a file on your computer. If you turn on sync, a copy goes to a hidden folder of your own Google Drive that only WE Budget can open, directly from your computer. There is no server of ours in between, no account with us, no analytics and no ads. Details are in the privacy policy.
